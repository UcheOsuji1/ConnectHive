## Rules preserved and proposed disclosures

Approved product prices are Plus $7.99/month or $69.99/year; Free $0. Paid Hive
prices are owner-configured monthly USD amounts with ongoing benefits. Plus is
separate from paid Hive membership; members need no Plus. Monetization requires
Plus, current Stripe verification and staff approval. Quotas remain one/three
created active Hives and five/unlimited free memberships for Free/Plus. Paid
memberships consume no free slots. Paid admission follows verified payment.

Before paid-Hive Checkout show the actual operator, recurring amount, monthly
renewal, ongoing benefits, owner cancellation terms, cancellation in Settings →
Billing & Subscriptions, paid-through access and owner refund responsibility.
The offer now includes the owner's profile name; staff must verify this reflects
the contracting operator, including business identity and contact information.
Never call a return redirect proof of payment. The activation screen waits for
verified server state. Cancellation stops future renewals after Stripe confirms;
access normally continues to paid-through. A saved but unconfirmed cancellation
is explicitly pending and retried. Full current-period refunds revoke refunded
access; partial refunds retain it. Disputes require review rather than automatic
irreversible revocation.

Draft Terms now clarify recorded refund requests, owner responses and Stripe
processing, escalation through platform reports/support, statutory consumer
rights, and retention/account-deletion review. Account deletion cannot erase
financial obligations. A response in TrueHive neither sends money nor waives
rights. Retention periods, lawful basis, contact channel and escalation deadlines
require approval before the draft is published as binding text.

## Professional compliance checklist

Launch jurisdiction is not confirmed. California/US is a **review assumption**,
not a conclusion about every customer's applicable law.

- Counsel: recurring-billing disclosures, affirmative consent evidence, durable
  acknowledgments/receipts, online cancellation, reminders and material changes.
  Evaluate California's amended automatic-renewal requirements effective July
  2025 against both Plus and owner offerings. Current in-app acknowledgment alone
  is not certification that every required consent/notice has been implemented.
  [California Attorney General guidance](https://www.oag.ca.gov/news/press-releases/attorney-general-bonta-issues-consumer-alert-california%E2%80%99s-automatic-renewal-law).
- Counsel: federal ROSCA/FTC Act, state consumer law and marketplace/operator
  representations. The 2024 FTC negative-option rule was vacated; do not treat
  that rule as current law. Review the actual applicable obligations and the
  [FTC rule docket](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/rules/negative-option-rule)
  and [2026 rulemaking notice](https://search.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/03/ftc-seeks-public-comment-response-advance-notice-proposed-rulemaking-regarding-negative-option).
- Counsel: owner agreement, lawful refund terms, chargeback responsibilities,
  complaint escalation, safety suspensions and refunds for unavailable benefits.
  Confirm accessible customer contact and owner identity before publication.
- Tax professional: sales-tax treatment of memberships/services, marketplace
  facilitator duties, nexus, information reporting, invoices, non-US restrictions
  and whether Stripe Tax or other tooling is required.
- Privacy counsel/security: Stripe/platform data roles, disclosures, retention
  schedule, deletion exceptions and least-privilege staff financial access.
- Business + counsel: approve final Terms and notices; then deliberately bump
  consent version and verify acceptance, receipts and cancellation notices.
  Email delivery has not been tested in this task.

No contractual draft purports to eliminate statutory consumer rights, Stripe
agreement obligations, or all platform liability.
